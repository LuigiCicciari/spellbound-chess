    How to play
    
      
      
        
    • Click one of your pieces, then click a glowing square. You can also drag, or type moves like Nf3.
      • 
        
    • Just want to see the battles? Press ▶ Watch.
      • 
        
    • After a game, open the 📜 Battle report: a chart of who was winning, your accuracy, your biggest mistakes with the better move, and your best move replayed.
      • 
        
    • Casual is for practice: take moves back, ask for a hint, and see which of your pieces are in danger (attacked and undefended, or attacked by a cheaper piece). Rated games count on the leaderboard, with no takebacks or hints. The mode is fixed once a game starts.
      • 
        
    • Pick a computer level. Each level is 400 rating points stronger than the last; levels 4–7 are powered by Stockfish. Beating stronger stones earns more rating.
      • 
        
    • The first time you finish a game, pick a name and how well you play; after that, rated games save to the Leaderboard automatically. You start even with the matching level (400, 800 or 1200). Ratings here come from games against the computer, so they're close to, but not the same as, chess.com or lichess ratings.
      • 
        
    • Turn on the Clock for timed games; running out of time loses (unless the other side can't checkmate, then it's a draw).
      • 
        
    • Animations → Cinematic swoops the camera in for big captures.
      • 
        
    • Playing on another device? Open ✎ and copy your recovery code, then paste it there.
      • 
        
    • ✦ Hint (when available) shows what real players played from this position.
