SPELLBOUND CHESS

Privacy policy · Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version: Spellbound Chess has no accounts, passwords, ads or tracking cookies, and never sells or shares your data for advertising. If you pick a leaderboard name, that name, your rating and your rated games are public. Visits are counted anonymously. You can delete everything from inside the game at any time.

Spellbound Chess is a free chess game made by Luigi Cicciari, played at luigicicciari.github.io/spellbound-chess, inside Tableau Public, and in any app version of the game. Questions or requests: spellbound@cicciari.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between visits: your settings (sound, view, level), your recent games and puzzle progress, a random player ID and a private key that proves the ID is yours, and a random ID used only to limit how often feedback can be sent. Nothing here is sent anywhere unless described below. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

If you choose a leaderboard name

Playing Casual, Watching, or doing the daily puzzle without a name sends nothing about you to the server. If you pick a name, the game saves the following on our database (hosted by Supabase):

  • Your player: the name you chose, your starting level choice, rating and record (wins, draws, losses), and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your private key.
  • Your rated games: the moves, result, how the game ended, the computer level, your color, clock setting, date and time, and the game version.
  • Your daily puzzle results: the day, when you started and finished, whether you solved it, and how many wrong moves you made.

Public: your name, rating, record, rated games and puzzle results appear on the leaderboards and can be read by anyone. Please use a nickname, not your full real name. Your private key is never shown.

If you send feedback

The 💬 Feedback box sends your message and the name you add (optional), plus technical details that help fix problems: device type, browser and its version string, screen and window size, whether you're in landscape, using touch, inside an embedded frame, in the 3D or flat board, or in home-screen mode, the game version, and what the game was doing (mode, level, phase, move count, puzzle day, whether you have a leaderboard name). If you have a leaderboard name, your player ID is included. It never includes your moves or your private key. Feedback is private: only the developer can read it.

Visit counts

The game uses GoatCounter to count visits. It records the page, the site you came from, browser and operating system, screen size category and country, plus a few anonymous events (a game was started or finished, Watch was opened, a puzzle was started or solved, feedback was opened, data was deleted). It uses no cookies and no unique identifiers that follow you, and it doesn't collect names, moves or player IDs. Ad blockers may block it; the game works the same.

Other services the game loads

To show the game, your browser downloads files from a few services, which receive your IP address and browser details the way any website does: GitHub Pages (the game itself), jsDelivr (the 3D library), and Google Fonts (the title font). Supabase and these services may keep short-lived technical logs. Daily puzzles come from the public Lichess puzzle database and are served with the game; Lichess is only contacted if you click a link to it. When the game is played inside Tableau Public, Tableau's own privacy policy applies to that page.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no selling or renting data.
  • No tracking cookies and no cross-site tracking.
  • No email addresses, phone numbers, locations, contacts or photos are collected.

Deleting your data

In the game, open ? (How to play) and choose Delete my data, or open the ✎ next to your name and choose Delete my data. This removes your player, rating, rated games, puzzle results and any feedback you sent, from the server and from your device, immediately. It can't be undone.

If you can't use the game (for example, you played on a device you no longer have), email spellbound@cicciari.com with your leaderboard name and we'll remove it within 30 days. Visit counts can't be deleted individually because they aren't linked to you.

Children

The game doesn't ask for personal information. Players choose their own nickname, which is public, so younger players should pick one that isn't their real name.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game.
